Privacy Policy

How AMAR'S WORLD collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: September 2026

Privacy Policy

AMAR'S WORLD ("we", "us", "our") provides education consultancy and application guidance to students considering study in the UK. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, or otherwise get in touch, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, country, academic background, preferred course and study destination, and any other details you choose to share with us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, offer guidance on courses and universities, support your application, and communicate with you about your study-abroad journey. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing of Information

We may share relevant details with universities or partner institutions only where necessary to support your application, and only with your knowledge. We may also use trusted third-party services (such as email or form-handling providers) solely to help us communicate with you.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to support your enquiry or application, or as required by law.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@amarsworld.com or amarsworld03@gmail.com.
