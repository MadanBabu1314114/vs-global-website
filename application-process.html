Home / Application Process

From enquiry to enrolment, one step at a time

Every stage involves a different combination of you, AMAR'S WORLD, your university and UK immigration authorities. Here's who does what.

You & AMAR'S WORLD

Enquiry

You share your goals and background with us through the enquiry form, WhatsApp or a call.

AMAR'S WORLD

Profile Assessment

We review your academic record, English test scores and work experience to understand realistic options.

You & AMAR'S WORLD

Course & University Selection

Together, we shortlist courses and universities that fit your profile, budget and goals.

You

Document Collection

You gather transcripts, certificates, English test results, references and other required documents.

AMAR'S WORLD

University Application

We help prepare and submit your application to your chosen universities.

University

University Assessment

The university reviews your application against its own entry requirements and criteria.

University

Offer Letter

If successful, the university issues a conditional or unconditional offer letter.

You & AMAR'S WORLD

Accept Offer & Complete Conditions

You accept your offer and work through any remaining conditions, with our guidance where useful.

You

Deposit / Fees, If Required

Some universities require a tuition deposit or fee payment at this stage — requirements vary by university.

You & AMAR'S WORLD

CAS Process

We guide you in preparing the information and documents your university needs to consider issuing your CAS.

University

CAS Issued by University

CAS is issued by the university, not by AMAR'S WORLD. This confirms your acceptance for immigration purposes.

You & AMAR'S WORLD

Check Visa Requirements

We help you understand the current student visa requirements that apply to your situation.

You

Prepare Evidence

You gather financial evidence and other supporting documents required for your visa application.

UKVI

Online Visa Application

You submit your student visa application through the official UK government website.

You

Pay Visa Fee & IHS, If Applicable

You pay the visa application fee and Immigration Health Surcharge where these apply to your case.

UKVI

Biometrics

You attend an appointment to provide biometric information as part of your visa application.

You

Submit Evidence

You submit any additional evidence requested to support your visa application.

UKVI

UKVI Processing

UK Visas and Immigration processes your application. Processing times can vary.

UKVI

Visa Decision

Visa decisions are made by the relevant UK immigration authorities — this outcome cannot be guaranteed by AMAR'S WORLD or the university.

UKVI

eVisa

If approved, you receive your immigration status digitally as an eVisa.

You

Travel

With your visa confirmed, you make travel arrangements to the UK, with pre-departure guidance from us.

University

Enrolment

You complete enrolment at your university and officially begin your programme.

This timeline is a general guide. Exact steps, order and requirements can vary by university, course and individual circumstances, and by current UK government policy.
