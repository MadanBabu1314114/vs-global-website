Document checklist

A general starting point for the documents commonly needed for UK university applications and student visas.

  • ✓Valid passport
  • ✓Degree certificate
  • ✓Academic transcripts
  • ✓English language test evidence
  • ✓CV / résumé
  • ✓Personal statement
  • ✓Academic or professional references
  • ✓Work experience documents (if applicable)
  • ✓Financial documents (proof of funds)
  • ✓TB test certificate (if required for your country)
  • ✓ATAS certificate (for certain courses, where required)
Document requirements vary by university, course and student circumstances. Students should follow the latest requirements provided by their university and the UK government.
