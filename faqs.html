Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how AMAR'S WORLD works, and what's decided by universities and UK authorities.

A consultancy can save time and reduce confusion by helping you understand your options, prepare a strong application and navigate the steps toward a UK student visa. We guide and support you — the final decisions still rest with universities and UK authorities.

CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) is a reference number confirming your place at a university for immigration purposes. It is issued by the university, not by AMAR'S WORLD, once you meet the required conditions.

No. Visa decisions are made solely by UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) and other relevant UK authorities. AMAR'S WORLD helps you prepare a complete and accurate application, but cannot guarantee or influence the outcome.

Yes, many students apply to a shortlist of universities. We help you build a balanced list based on your profile and preferences.

Commonly required documents include your passport, degree certificate, transcripts, English test evidence, CV, personal statement and references. See our Document Checklist — exact requirements vary by university and course.

Many universities accept alternative English tests, and some offer exemptions depending on your academic background. We can help you understand which options apply to your situation.
